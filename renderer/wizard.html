Pick your dictation hotkey

One key combination toggles everything: press to start dictating, press again to stop and transcribe.

The default works fine for most people — click the box to choose your own. On GNOME/KDE Wayland, apps may not be able to grab global keys — instead, bind a system keyboard shortcut to earheart --toggle.

Choose a microphone

The system default is usually right. Pick a specific device if you have more than one.

Automatic uses the system default when it is allowed, otherwise another allowed microphone, and falls back to the system default if none is.

Music, videos and calls get quieter while the microphone is capturing, and come back when you stop, pause or cancel.

Speech becomes text on this computer

Earheart runs NVIDIA's Parakeet model right inside the app — nothing to install, no separate program.

  • Your voice never leaves this computer
  • Faster than real time, even without a graphics card
  • Understands 25 languages automatically

Prefer a remote service (OpenAI, Groq, your own server)? You can switch to one anytime in Settings.

Polish your transcripts automatically?

After transcription, a small language model can fix punctuation and capitalization and remove filler words and false starts — so what lands is clean, not a raw stream of speech.

Runs a language model inside the app (Granite 4.0 Micro by default) — like an editor that tidies every dictation for you, fully offline.
